Fragrance Guide

Learn the basics of fragrance and what to look for when choosing a cologne.

Fragrance Notes

Fragrances contain top, middle, and base notes that develop over time.

Scent Families

Common fragrance families include fresh, woody, citrus, sweet, and spicy scents.

Concentrations

EDT, EDP, and parfum contain different concentrations of fragrance oils, which can affect strength and longevity.

Performance and Use

Longevity and projection describe how long a fragrance lasts and how strongly it can be noticed. Season and occasion can also affect when a fragrance works best.
